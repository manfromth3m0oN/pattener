    Curves

    Edges are necklines, hems, cuffs and leg openings; close each loop. Seams split the garment into pieces that lie flat; their ends snap onto an edge or seam within 2 cm. Pick the tool above the 3D view and click on the body; drag a point of the selected curve to move it.

      Ease zones

      A zone has its own ease within its radius, blending back to the rest towards the edge: higher (e.g. 0.92) is looser, lower is tighter.

        Latex sheets

        The sheets you'll cut from. Each piece uses the default sheet unless you paint it another one (Paint tool above the 3D view). Leave Ease empty to use the pattern's ease or the swatch calibration.
