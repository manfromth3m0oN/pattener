Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. Orange lines show where each supplied girth is measured.

    Garment

    Draw on the body. Edges are necklines, hems, cuffs and leg openings; close each loop. Seams split the garment into pieces that lie flat; their ends snap onto an edge or seam within 2 cm. Curves pass smoothly through the points you click; drag a point of the selected curve to move it. Then pick a point inside the garment.

      Ease zones

      A zone has its own ease within its radius, blending back to the rest towards the edge: higher (e.g. 0.92) is looser, lower is tighter.

      bridging40+ mmHg

      How hard the latex presses on the body. Blue: it bridges over a hollow and won't touch the skin. Red: it presses hard (15–20 mmHg is light compression; over 40 is very firm). Updated by Make pattern. Red rings mark where a piece has to distort to lie flat: a dart or seam towards the ring (or a set-in sleeve, at an armpit) would help.
